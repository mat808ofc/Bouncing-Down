# Bouncing-Down
Made for Haven Lisbon!!

**What is it about?**
- It's basically a platformer. Daven falls in a cave and your job is to rescue him. Gather as much crystals as you can but watch out for some crystals that kill you. And go fast!! Your final score depends on time and crystals collected! Good luck on rescuing Daven!!
- Made in Godot.
- Made by: Mateus L, Francisco B, Tomás C. with the help of the Haven Lisbon team!!!
